Long Silent Retreat

For practitioners ready to sit with fewer interruptions and more depth. Ten days of extended noble silence, private teacher sessions, and a schedule built around sustained, quiet practice.

10 nights Private room 2 private sessions

Your Retreat

Room to go further.

Ten days gives the practice time to move past the surface. This retreat is built for guests who have already sat a shorter retreat and want fewer interruptions, longer sitting blocks, and more one-on-one time with a teacher to work through what comes up.

Extended noble silence runs for the full stay, with two private teacher sessions scheduled across the ten days — one early, to set direction, and one near the end, to help you integrate what you've found.

Day by Day

How the ten days unfold

1

Arrival & first teacher session

Check in, settle into your private room, and meet one-on-one with a resident teacher to set intentions before noble silence begins.

2–4

Settling in

Longer sitting blocks and walking meditation, with the initial restlessness of arrival giving way to a steadier rhythm.

5–8

Sustained depth practice

The core of the retreat — extended silent sitting and walking, nightly guidance, and space to work through whatever the stillness surfaces.

9

Second teacher session

A private conversation to help translate ten days of practice into something you can carry home.

10

Closing & departure

Noble silence lifts after the final sitting, followed by a group closing circle and checkout by 11 AM.
